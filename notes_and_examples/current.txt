Final Workflow for DAG Synchronization

    On Receiving a Proposal:
        Validate the proposal size.
        Wait until the DAG reaches the required round before sending prevote.

    On Sending Prevote:
        Ensure local DAG is in sync for one round lower than the prevote round.

    On Handling DAG Sync Requests:
        Handle external sync requests with handle_dag_sync but avoid redundant checks.

    On Reconstructing and Committing:
        Ensure all parent units are received and committed before multicasting commit messages.

By restructuring these calls, you'll align closer with the ch-RBC protocol, reducing redundancy and improving efficiency.
