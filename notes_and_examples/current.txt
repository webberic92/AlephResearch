🧠 Observation on Timeout Failures

Node 29 is failing to send prevotes to the second half of the list (starting around 10.0.147.81) despite successful connections earlier.

This is likely:

    TCP exhaustion (ephemeral ports used up)

    Per-destination rate limiting or SYN flood protection on the receiving EC2 instances

    Or AWS VPC-level connection throttling

✅ Suggestion:

    Add logging to track open connections.

    Insert a short delay (tokio::time::sleep) every X sends, or use a Semaphore to rate-limit concurrent HTTP requests.

    Investigate with ulimit -n, and raise it to at least 65536 in your startup script.
